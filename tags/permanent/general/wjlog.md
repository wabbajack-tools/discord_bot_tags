## Wabbajack Log File

If you're asked to provide your Wabbajack log, please upload `Wabbajack.current.log` to the appropriate support channel.

**How to find your log:**

1. Click **Open Log Folder** in Wabbajack, as shown in the screenshot below.
2. Locate `Wabbajack.current.log`.
3. Drag and drop the file directly into the Discord channel.

**Alternative method:**

If you cannot access the button, navigate to the `logs` folder inside your Wabbajack version directory, located alongside `Wabbajack.exe`.

For example: `C:\Wabbajack\3.7.4.1\logs\Wabbajack.current.log`

-# Your installation location and version number may differ.

**Please upload the complete `.log` rather than screenshots or selected excerpts. The full log provides the context needed to identify the cause of a failure.**
