## No Access to a Discord Channel

If a channel appears as `🔒 No Access`, you may not have the required role or permissions to view it.

For publicly accessible support channels, check the server's **Channels & Roles** section or other available role-selection channels and select the relevant game or modlist role, if offered.

If you still cannot access the channel, check the server rules or ask for assistance in an accessible support channel.

Some channels are restricted to specific roles and cannot be unlocked through self-assigned roles.
