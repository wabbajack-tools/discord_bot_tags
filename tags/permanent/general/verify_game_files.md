## Verify Your Game Files

Your base game files may not match what Wabbajack expects. Verifying them through your game launcher can restore missing, modified, or corrupted files.

**Important:** Verification may replace modified game files. Back up any manual modifications you wish to preserve.

**Note:** Not all storefront versions are compatible with every modlist. Always check the modlist's requirements before installing.

**Close Wabbajack**, then follow the instructions for your storefront:

**Steam**
1. Open your Steam Library.
2. Right-click your game and select **Properties**.
3. Select **Installed Files → Verify integrity of game files**.

**GOG GALAXY**
1. Select your game in GOG GALAXY.
2. Click the customization button next to **Play**.
3. Select **Manage installation → Verify / Repair**.

**Epic Games Store**
1. Open your Epic Games Library.
2. Click the three dots next to your game.
3. Select **Manage → Verify**.

**EA app**
1. Open your Library and locate your game.
2. Click the **Manage** icon.
3. Select **Repair**.

Once verification is complete, restart Wabbajack and resume the installation using the same installation and downloads directories.

You will not lose your existing Wabbajack download progress by verifying your game files.
