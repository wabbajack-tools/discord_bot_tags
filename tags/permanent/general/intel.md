## Intel 13th/14th Generation CPU Instability

Some Intel 13th- and 14th-generation desktop processors are affected by a known instability issue that may cause crashes during demanding workloads, including Wabbajack installations.

**If your system is affected:**
1. Update your motherboard BIOS to the latest stable version provided by its manufacturer.
2. Ensure the BIOS includes Intel microcode `0x12F` or later.
3. Apply **Intel Default Settings** where supported.
4. Check whether the system remains unstable under other demanding workloads.

If instability continues after applying the recommended updates, contact your processor or system manufacturer about further diagnostics and warranty options.

**A Wabbajack crash does not, by itself, indicate processor degradation.**
